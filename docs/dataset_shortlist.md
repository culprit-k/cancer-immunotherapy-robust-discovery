# Dataset shortlist and locked cohort selection

**Last checked:** 25 October 2026  
**Status:** HCC is selected as the biological study system, but the released normalized TIFFs are **not compatible with direct pretrained VirTues inference**. The archive and panel have been downloaded/audited in CI; the model checkpoint has been immutably pinned. Use this cohort only with an author-supplied original-scale route or a separately prespecified normalization-domain adaptation.

## Selected study system

### Development cohort — HCC discovery IMC (54 patients)

- Zenodo: https://zenodo.org/records/10793609; DOI 10.5281/zenodo.10793609.
- Modality: imaging mass cytometry (IMC); the linked study describes a 41-plex panel.
- Data: normalized per-channel multistack TIFF images, cell masks, stromal masks, and `DCCohort_panel.csv`.
- Rights: the NLM Dataset Catalog reports **CC BY 4.0** for this record: https://datasetcatalog.nlm.nih.gov/dataset?q=0002065446. Use with attribution and the required indication of changes; preserve a copy of this metadata in study records.
- Role: development data are available for a documented HCC-specific normalization-domain experiment, but **not** for direct inference through the pretrained VirTues default preprocessing pipeline. If a new adaptation is designed, split by patient before tuning and never let external ICI cohort information influence calibration.

### Untouched external evaluation cohort — HCC ICI cohort (42 patients)

- Zenodo: https://zenodo.org/records/10794904; DOI 10.5281/zenodo.10794904.
- Data: normalized channel-wise TIFF stacks, cell masks, stroma masks, and `ICICohort_panel.csv`; patient metadata are in Supplementary Table 3 of the associated paper.
- Rights: the completed Zenodo API metadata audit reported `cc-by-4.0` for this record; the landing page does not visibly display the field. Preserve the metadata-only audit evidence in [run 40210741291](https://github.com/culprit-k/cancer-immunotherapy-robust-discovery/actions/runs/40210741291), and confirm with the data contact/institutional office if policy requires the licence to be shown on the landing page.
- Role: potentially untouched external evaluation after the model/input pipeline is fixed using discovery-only data. Do not use this cohort to select targets, tune thresholds, define endpoints or calibrate abstention rules. Its input must use the same explicitly validated preprocessing-domain design.

Associated paper: Salié et al., *Gut*, DOI https://doi.org/10.1136/gutjnl-2024-332837. It describes a 54-patient discovery cohort and independent 42-patient ICI cohort, using a 41-plex IMC panel to study CD8 T-cell infiltration and immune states involving PD-1, TIM-3/LAG-3, PD-L1-positive myeloid/APC states and CD204 macrophages.

## Selected reconstruction checkpoint variant

**Official repository:** https://github.com/bunnelab/virtues  
**Model hub:** https://huggingface.co/bunnelab/virtues  
**Variant/file:** `virtues-imc14/model.safetensors`  
**Weights licence:** **CC BY-NC 4.0**; non-commercial use only.

The official VirTues model table says `virtues-imc14` was trained on 14 IMC datasets. The paper distinguishes this core corpus from its extended 32-cohort corpus, which adds seven further IMC cohorts (refs. 90–96) as well as CODEX, Orion and MIBI cohorts. The Salié HCC paper appears as reference 95 among the extended-corpus additions. Accordingly, `virtues-imc14` is the strongest published candidate for this IMC cohort pair. `virtues-sp32` must not be used for a held-out Salié claim because it includes the HCC study. The exact cohort omitted from `virtues-sp31` was not verified here, so that checkpoint is not approved for held-out HCC use unless its training inventory confirms exclusion.

## Compatibility and remaining scientific gate

The cohort pair is biologically relevant, but a modality match alone does not establish model compatibility. The completed discovery audit found normalized TIFFs and masks, not original MCD acquisitions or the quantile/mean/SD tables expected by VirTues. The HCC paper states that PENGUIN normalizes image channels into [0,1] and applies background removal and per-marker percentile denoising. VirTues expects a different clipping/log/blur/tissue-statistics standardization path. Merely converting TIFFs to the spora directory layout would not resolve this representation mismatch. Direct pretrained inference is NO-GO unless the original-scale representation is supplied or a separate adaptation is trained and validated.

VirTues reconstructs marker channels; it does not reproduce Morpheus target discovery. HCC is not Morpheus's original melanoma/colorectal task. After download, inspect both panel CSVs, reconcile image/ROI/patient identifiers, define a reproducible CD8 endpoint, and document a reproduced or explicitly adapted counterfactual baseline. Final target markers must come from the actual panel CSVs, fixed before external evaluation; never perturb the CD8 endpoint channel to predict CD8 infiltration.

## Deprioritized alternatives

- Rigamonti 2024 NSCLC IMC: CD8 annotations are useful for smoke testing, but its 24-marker panel does not obviously support the intended target set.
- Hoch et al. melanoma IMC: biologically relevant, but VirTues directly evaluates cell typing on the cohort.
- Xiao et al. melanoma IMC: includes CD8a/PD-1/PD-L1 but appears in the extended VirTues training corpus.
- Godbole et al. 2026 melanoma IMC: interesting panel, but only six patients and no explicit licence displayed.
- Other spora cohorts remain candidates only after panel, rights and checkpoint-exposure audits.
