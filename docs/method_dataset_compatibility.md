## Decision consequences

### Cross-method intervention decision utility
Current obstacle: Morpheus outputs perturbations to molecular channels and predicted CD8+ infiltration; SPAD-CFR generates spatial tissue counterfactuals; the MICCAI method estimates directional cell-cell influence; PHAROS ranks drug combinations in single-cell state space. Those output objects are not interchangeable.

Candidate A should proceed only if two methods can be evaluated on the same patient-level data or both can be mapped to a well-justified common endpoint *before* inspecting results. If not, a head-to-head comparison would be scientifically invalid.

### Cross-cohort/platform transfer
Current obstacle: matching cancer labels or target names does not establish comparability. We need shared measured markers, a consistent signed intervention interpretation, compatible patient-level endpoint, and genuinely independent samples. Same-patient or same-study reuse can create leakage if provenance is not checked.

Candidate B should proceed only after a channel/marker crosswalk and endpoint-compatibility check.

## Immediate deliverable from this matrix

The next step should be a small data dictionary/crosswalk for candidate resources and exact access terms. No model training or transfer result should be claimed until the files are available, checksummed, inspected and a baseline evaluated on the chosen endpoint.

## Current recommendation from the compatibility audit

Do not pursue broad cross-method comparisons or generic cross-platform transfer as the headline. The new candidate is a masked-marker **counterfactual recommendation-fidelity** study. Select the actual cohort only after checking channels and rights. A good initial cohort must have a measured CD8/T-cell label, enough immune-related measured markers to define a target budget, patient-level IDs, and whole-channel holdout ground truth. If the public spora corpus has no cohort satisfying these rules, stop rather than force an invalid comparison.

## Updated novelty boundary after inspecting VirTues Methods

VirTues already evaluates downstream cell-phenotyping stability after reconstructing masked markers: it removes CD20/CD38 for B-cell classification and progressively removes CD3E/CD4/CD45RA/CD8 for T-cell phenotyping, then reruns the phenotyping task. Therefore, "virtual-marker reconstruction preserves downstream analysis" is too broad to claim as new. The candidate is specifically about **counterfactual target-set and predicted-objective fidelity**, which is a different downstream decision from phenotype classification. The complete supplement and a broader prior-art search still need review before calling this an established gap.


### Most promising initial cohort for the exact downstream test

The spora catalogue lists rigamonti2024integrating as 108 patients / 158 IMC images with H&E. Its published benchmark configuration includes explicit CD4 T-cell and CD8 T-cell annotation labels. The VirTues paper describes a leave-one-dataset-out marker-reconstruction evaluation on Rigamonti et al. This makes it the strongest candidate for a downstream recommendation-fidelity pilot—if the candidate intervention channels exist and the correct leave-one-dataset-out checkpoint or retraining procedure is available. Do not assume the default released VirTues checkpoint is trained without this cohort; verify its training-data inventory first.

The hoch2022multiplexed melanoma/chemokine cohort (35 patients / 50 IMC images) is more directly relevant biologically, but VirTues already performs cell-typing evaluation on it and training exposure may complicate independent validation. It should be treated as a development cohort or evaluated using a suitably held-out reconstruction checkpoint.

## Verified VirTues implementation compatibility gate (25 October 2026)

The official VirTues repository implementation was inspected after the HCC panel audit. The dataset loader in `virtues/data/multiplex_dataset.py` documents and applies the following steps in order: per-tissue/channel 99th-percentile clipping, `log1p`, Gaussian blur, and per-tissue log-standardization using quantiles, means and standard deviations. It also filters channels based on whether their `protein_id` has a matching marker embedding. Its channel metadata is therefore more than a list of display names. The official README specifies marker embeddings named by UniProt ID and identifies the published weights as model-specific licenses. Sources: [VirTues repository](https://github.com/bunnelab/virtues), [VirTues dataset loader](https://github.com/bunnelab/virtues/blob/main/virtues/data/multiplex_dataset.py), [VirTues README/model table](https://github.com/bunnelab/virtues#models).

The HCC archive is described as containing **preprocessed and normalized** multichannel TIFFs and masks, while its discovered panel provides `Channel`, `MetalTag` and `Target` only. The source paper further states that raw channels were arcsinh-transformed with cofactor 0.5 for downstream analysis, while normalized channels were used for clustering. This differs from VirTues' documented 99th-percentile clipping → `log1p` → Gaussian blur → per-tissue log-standardization pipeline. The Zenodo archive does not advertise raw MCD acquisitions or VirTues' required quantile/mean/std tables. Thus the input mismatch is concrete, not merely hypothetical. **Do not apply VirTues preprocessing blindly to already-normalized values and do not feed the TIFF channels to the checkpoint by channel position alone.** We must establish whether the archive retains compatible preprocessed intensities or recover the original intensity scale and per-tissue statistics; map each target to a canonical protein/UniProt identifier; preserve exact channel order; confirm pixel resolution and crop conventions; and create the required quantile/mean/std metadata. Any required conversion must be validated against the official reconstruction demo on a known-compatible example first.

The selected `virtues-imc14` checkpoint is listed by the official model table as trained on 14 IMC datasets and has **no segmentation head**. It may be a reasonable candidate for marker reconstruction, but it does not itself supply cell segmentation/phenotyping labels or a validated CD8-infiltration endpoint. The current HCC masks are cell/stroma masks; they must not be treated as CD8 labels without checking their semantics and source annotations. A target-discovery stage must be separately reproduced or specified, with a fixed objective and allowed intervention markers, before any recommendation-fidelity result is interpretable.

### Required gates before a real-data inference run

1. **Input-format gate:** document archive intensity scale, normalization history, pixel size, TIFF axis/channel order, masks, per-tissue quantiles, means and standard deviations. If information required by the checkpoint cannot be recovered, stop and choose a compatible dataset or a method that accepts this format.
2. **Marker identity gate:** create a 42-row mapping from `Target`/metal tag/channel to canonical protein name and UniProt ID; record aliases, mapping source, ambiguity and whether the checkpoint's embedding directory supports every marker. No silent alias guessing.
3. **Model contract gate:** pin the source-code revision as well as weight revision/hash, follow the official inference notebook, load the exact model configuration and marker embeddings, and confirm output shapes/channel order on a known-compatible example.
4. **Exposure gate:** resolve whether the exact HCC accession or closely related source cohort appears in any pretraining/fine-tuning data used for the checkpoint. The label `imc14` alone is not accession-level proof.
5. **Endpoint gate:** define CD8 infiltration from documented cell labels or a validated, prespecified phenotype rule. If no defensible cell-level labels exist, change the endpoint to a supported marker reconstruction/phenotype outcome rather than inventing CD8 truth.
6. **Counterfactual gate:** reproduce the baseline method or clearly specify a defensible alternative, intervention semantics, allowed targets and directions, target budget, and evaluation objective. Predicted uplift must remain labelled model-predicted, not a treatment effect.
7. **Pilot gate:** run one compatible image and one masked marker, verify finite outputs, scale, spatial alignment and reconstruction metrics against the truly measured channel. No held-out patient experiment until this passes.

**Current execution decision: NO-GO for HCC/VirTues inference.** The published HCC images are already normalized, and the paper's arcsinh processing is not interchangeable with VirTues' clipping/log1p/standardization. Do not attempt to invert the transformation from normalized TIFFs. First determine whether original MCD/raw intensity data and acquisition metadata are available under suitable terms. If not, switch to a dataset with compatible original-scale images and required metadata, or choose a reconstruction method designed for the available normalized format. This is a data-contract blocker, not a failure of the research question.

## Final HCC preprocessing/exposure gate — 25 October 2026

- **Archive audit:** PASS. Successful run [40210741291](https://github.com/culprit-k/cancer-immunotherapy-robust-discovery/actions/runs/40210741291) verifies MD5 `a842fa253ac2f0eda3cbf4d41e1b0ec1`, size 1,541,132,381 bytes and SHA-256 `8d09d946659164bb2275468ca4ad3239819b2036af83e63ab7b586ddbfc47f72`. It parsed 42 panel rows; all structural checks passed.
- **Preprocessing:** NO-GO for direct pretrained VirTues inference on the released HCC TIFFs. The HCC source states that PENGUIN normalizes channels into [0,1], removes background and applies marker-specific percentile denoising per ROI. VirTues' official loader instead clips at a tissue-specific 99th percentile, applies `log1p`, blurs, and standardizes with tissue-specific means and SDs. The transformations and representation are not equivalent; do not invert or invent statistics.
- **Checkpoint exposure:** The pinned official `virtues-imc14` revision `43fd00fce052c46f9e0cc1a87f42806620632319`, file SHA-256 `714efed862f4a4b5cfd13f91e1ab8a7589f1295bd1735f827b4f8d4806cde28f`, is the appropriate public variant for an HCC held-out question. The VirTues Methods classify Salié et al. as ref. 95 among seven extra IMC cohorts included in the extended corpus, not the core 14 used for `virtues-imc14`. This is strong publication-level evidence of no direct study inclusion, but not a machine-readable certificate mapping Zenodo accessions to an individual training job. The extended `virtues-sp32` and `virtues-sp31` checkpoints are not valid held-out choices.
- **What remains open:** original-scale HCC data; a validated cell-label/CD8 endpoint; canonical marker-embedding mapping; and a reproducible counterfactual baseline. No model inference or biological result has been produced.
