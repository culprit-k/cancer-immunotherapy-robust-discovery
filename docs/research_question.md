# Research question and scope

## Working title

**Robustness-Aware Computational Discovery of Cancer Immunotherapy Targets**

Subtitle for manuscript planning: *Evaluating the stability of predicted molecular target combinations across patient samples and modelling assumptions.*

## Primary question

When computational models propose molecular target combinations associated with improved antitumour immune activity, can a prespecified robustness evaluation identify recommendations that remain stable across patient-level resampling and independent evaluation data?

## Hypothesis to test

Some candidate combinations may appear promising under one training sample or modelling configuration but change substantially under reasonable perturbations to the discovery process. A systematic combination-level stability analysis may reveal this fragility.

This hypothesis must be tested. It is not yet evidence of a research gap.

## Scope

- Computational methods and reproducible analysis only in the initial phase.
- Candidate targets are hypotheses generated by published models or our own analysis, not treatment recommendations.
- Focus on the stability of whole combinations, alongside individual-target stability.
- Evaluate generalization only where independent patients/cohorts and compatible measurements are available.
- Treat spatial protein measurements, transcriptomic response cohorts, and other modalities as distinct unless a justified harmonization strategy exists.

## Out of scope for the initial phase

- Clinical recommendations or patient-level medical decisions.
- Claims of treatment efficacy without experimental evidence.
- Claims that an algorithm or target is novel before a literature and prior-art review.
- Patentability conclusions.

## Key definitions

- **Target stability:** consistency with which a molecular target is selected across repeated discovery runs.
- **Combination stability:** consistency of the full proposed target set, including overlap/Jaccard-based measures.
- **External generalization:** performance on independent patients or cohorts not used for discovery or model selection.
- **Robustness:** insensitivity of a conclusion to prespecified, scientifically reasonable changes in sampling or modelling.
- **Biological validity:** independent evidence that a predicted perturbation has the proposed biological effect; computational stability alone does not establish this.

## Decision gate

Proceed to method development only if the audit identifies a specific limitation in existing work, suitable data support a valid test, and the proposed method can be compared against credible baselines.
